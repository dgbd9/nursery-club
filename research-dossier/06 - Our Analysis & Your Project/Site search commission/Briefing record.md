## Search instructions to include in the final master prompt

- Search by building history and business/ownership change as well as commercial listing categories. Include former offices, training/conference centres, hotels, leisure venues, schools/colleges, suitable civic/community/institutional buildings and substantial halls or estates, where the evidence supports a plausible fit.
- Examine both marketed and unmarketed leads. Local closure news, receivership/administration announcements, public-sector disposal registers, planning/licensing applications, historic brochures and Google/Maps business listings are discovery routes, not proof of availability.
- Follow each property's identity across former names, trading brands and owners. Verify rebranding, acquisition, reopening and competing redevelopment before promoting a closed venue as a lead.
- Preserve area definitions: site acreage, building footprint, GIA, NIA, lettable area and nursery room space are different measures. Record exactly what the source states. Do not infer usable area from room counts, photographs or a map polygon.
- Evaluate the actual family catchment and convenient repeated visits, not merely travel time to central London. Use published demographic and housing evidence where available. Distinguish straight-line proximity from travel time.
- Assess light, practical floorplates, entrances, nursery separation, garden access, circulation, accessibility, servicing, extraction, noise sensitivity and potential support-space sharing. Report unknowns; do not claim planning or building feasibility from marketing text.
- For bigger properties, distinguish the whole estate from any evidenced available wing, building or parcel. Do not assume a landlord will subdivide or fund works. Keep oversized speculative opportunities outside the core shortlist.
- For each lead, separate **physical fit**, **commercial/transaction route**, **delivery constraints** and **evidence confidence**. Unknown rent, ownership or planning information should lead to a targeted follow-up, not invented figures.
- Record asking price/rent, tenure, lease term, service charges, rates and other costs only where stated; preserve dates, VAT qualifiers and area bases. Do not equate an affordable asking price with affordability of the whole project.
- Check official heritage, planning and licensing records. Heritage status and possible community-value restrictions require specific investigation. Do not assume an existing use authorises the entire proposed hybrid operation.
- Return a ranked actionable shortlist, a separate conditional/off-market pipeline, and an exclusion/reference log. Provide direct source links, relevant dates, photographs/floorplan links and the most useful next question for each candidate. Do not pad the shortlist with attractive buildings that cannot meet the accommodation brief.
- Save core sources, register provenance and flag access gaps in the project dossier when filesystem access exists. Otherwise supply a structured source register and downloadable source links; never claim that a URL alone is an offline archive.
- Work read-only: no third-party contact, paid records, bookings, submissions or uploads without a further instruction. Do not use Firecrawl under this project's restrictions.
