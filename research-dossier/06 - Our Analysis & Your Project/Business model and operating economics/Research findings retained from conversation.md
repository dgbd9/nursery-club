# Research findings retained from the conversation

Summary filed 24 November 2026. This preserves the main research conclusions, not the full conversation. Source IDs resolve through the [source register](../../00%20-%20Start%20Here/Source%20register.md). Figures must be checked against originals before underwriting or investment use.

## What the business combines

OurHouse combines recurring club subscriptions with hospitality, family leisure, short-session childcare, fitness and coworking. The original nursery alongside Jaego's is a substantial operating business in its own right. A nursery-plus-club investment therefore needs separate nursery and club economics plus a combined site view, with shared costs counted once. Sources: CM01–CM02, NU01, KN22–KN24.

The nursery's existence does not establish instant cash generation. Kensal House Nursery's accounts show an initial accumulated deficit of £239,330 in 2022, 2023 turnover of £1,835,094 and operating loss of £14,272, then 2024 turnover of £2,588,526 and operating profit of £262,703. The 2024 operating margin is about 10.1%. These are nursery-company accounting results, not combined club/site EBITDA or cash flow. Sources: KN22–KN24.

## Original funding and ownership

The parent was incorporated as The Little Houses Group Limited, company 12926635, in October 2020. Founder Charles Gardiner originally held the single issued ordinary share. Nominal capital is not the startup investment budget. Sources: CH01–CH02.

The October 2022 confirmation statement records 907 issued shares: Gardiner 761 ordinary; Olivia Rostron 65 A; Nigel Gardiner 42 A; Stepper Point Limited 18 ordinary; Yakov Estraikh and Nicholas Crosthwaite 6 ordinary each; Jessica Russell 5 ordinary; Herbert Russell 4 ordinary. Ordinary shares carried votes and A shares did not; Gardiner held 95.125% of those ordinary-share votes. These are issued interests, not a fully diluted economic waterfall. Sources: CH04, CH07.

December 2022 documents identify £3 million of secured convertible loan notes associated with Imbiba. Group security covered the parent and subsidiaries; the notes were subsequently converted. The British Business Bank announcement's £90 million refers to the Imbiba fund, not an investment of that amount in Little Houses. Sources: CH03, CH08–CH09, FI01.

The October 2023 statement records Imbiba with 440 voting preferred shares and 398,000 non-voting preference shares, giving about 35.5% of the recorded share votes; Gardiner held about 55.8% of votes. Preferred economics and exit rights prevent treating voting percentages as a complete economic cap table. Sources: CH10–CH11 and relevant articles. Complete funding sources, loan terms and economic waterfall remain open questions.

## Nursery, club and property are not always one building

Kensal nursery was described as alongside Jaego's; Parsons House Nursery describes itself as across from Jesse's. A campus/adjacent-premises structure is relevant. Ofsted reported 98 places at Kensal and 95 at Parsons in the reports reviewed; children on roll do not establish FTE occupancy. Sources: NU01, NU03, OF01R–OF02R.

The May 2024 security schedule records 20-year Portobello Dock leases dated February 2022, and 15-year Heathmans Road leases entered in June/July 2023. Levy's property case study describes leasing and a later freehold transaction. Property purchase consideration must not be used as a club fit-out benchmark. Sources: CH12, PR01.

## Pre-launch marketing: what is and is not established

Jaego's had published publicity by October 2021, before its November 2022 opening. Contemporary coverage already referred to a building; we did not establish marketing before a location was secured. Pramstead published registration, membership-release and launch-price material; Restaurant Online covered the hospitality offer before opening. Sources: MK01–MK05.

Jesse's later used registration/early-access publicity and company social posts; an agency also published a launch announcement. These sources establish examples of activity, not the whole campaign or original Jaego's agency attribution. Sources: MK06–MK08. We did not establish marketing spend, paid-channel allocation, lead counts, conversion rates or CAC.
