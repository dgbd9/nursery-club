## Nursery scale and revenue

2,000–3,000 sq ft is about 186–279 m² of usable children's rooms. The user has operational experience indicating that a 70–90-place nursery can support £1m+ annual turnover at moderate occupancy. That is a credible proposition to test with their own data, not a claim of guaranteed turnover.

Use: **registered operating capacity × average FTE occupancy × annual realised revenue per occupied FTE place**. For illustration only, at an assumed 70% FTE occupancy and £18,000 annual realised revenue per occupied FTE:

| Places | Occupied FTE at 70% | Illustrative annual turnover |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 70 | 49 | £882,000 |
| 80 | 56 | £1,008,000 |
| 90 | 63 | £1,134,000 |

The occupancy and £18,000 yield are illustrative inputs, not researched local market benchmarks. Realised yield must reflect private/funded-hour mix, attendance patterns, funded weeks, discounts and collectible charges. Use the operator's actual data before adopting these inputs. Turnover is not a proxy for staffing contribution, EBITDA or cash available to support the club.

Nursery capacity within the room-area range depends on age mix and usable layout. Do not scale a 111-place older-child-heavy design proportionately to a baby-heavy setting. Apply the relevant EYFS usable-space rules (RG02) and a real room schedule. The property search must allow the nursery's full ancillary footprint beyond the stated children's rooms.

## Functions and measurements to preserve at smaller scale

1. **Recurring nursery demand and contribution:** FTE occupancy by room/age, realised yield per child-day, staffing deployment and contribution after a fair premises/support allocation.
2. **Recurring club subscriptions:** paying accounts, adult/child mix, net recurring revenue, retention and activation. Distinguish total enrolled members from people present at one time.
3. **A useful everyday visit:** comfortable hospitality/social space, children's play and a bookable offer that lets parents spend time together or apart. Decide which functions require dedicated rooms and which can share a flexible studio.
4. **Service capacity:** peak simultaneous attendance, café seats, play-space loading and bookable childcare/studio hours. A member-count ratio alone cannot establish how well the offer works at weekends or popular times.
5. **Shared operating support:** food production, staff facilities, reception/security, storage, cleaning and management. Allocate once in the combined view and fairly between nursery and club in department views; do not assume either subsidises the other without measuring it.
