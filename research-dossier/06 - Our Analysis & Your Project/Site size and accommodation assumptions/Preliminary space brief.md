This document preserves the earlier combined-site illustration; it is not the current property search brief. See [Current operating concept](Current%20operating%20concept.md).

Recorded from the sizing conversation, 24 November 2026.

For 50–60 day-nursery places plus a compact hospitality and family-club offer, the preliminary search allowance discussed was **8,000–12,000 sq ft internal**, plus suitable outdoor provision. A nursery with a café/lounge alone could be smaller; a pool and dedicated large gym were not included in the compact concept. This is our preliminary allowance, not a measured OurHouse benchmark, approved capacity or costed design.

All rows are working allocations. Circulation, food preparation, accessibility, escape, acoustics, staff facilities, simultaneous club users and nursery age mix need testing against a real building. Outdoor space, parking and terraces are additional considerations.

The EYFS activity-space rules discussed were 3.5 m² per under-two, 2.5 m² per two-year-old and 2.3 m² per child aged three to five. They apply to qualifying usable space, excluding specified ancillary areas. An illustrative 60-place mix of 15 under-twos, 20 two-year-olds and 25 older children gives 160 m² of qualifying activity space; that is not the whole nursery footprint. Source RG02, paragraph 3.83 and footnote 67. No fixed statutory outdoor square-metre ratio was asserted.

Historical company licensing material advertised approximately 20,000 sq ft for Jaego's and 30,000 sq ft for Jesse's, but contained placeholders for nursery floor areas. Published member floorplans are undimensioned and do not establish a reliable nursery/club percentage split. Future club marketing of crèches/kids clubs does not establish a full-day nursery. Sources LC01 and FP01–FP03.
