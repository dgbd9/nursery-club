## Nursery — 11 Heathmans Road

Pages 94–95 contain nursery first/ground-floor plans, with corresponding vector drawings on pp34–35. The nursery drawings are marked DRAFT. Latest listed revisions are 27 July 2023: first floor revision C, drawing 073-02-20-101-P2; ground floor revision G, drawing 073-02-20-100-P2.

| Children's space | Annotated area | Drawing's number of children |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Babies | 49 m² | 14 |
| 2–3 years, rooms of 55 + 46 m² | 101 m² | 40 |
| 3–5 years, rooms of 59 + 72 m² | 131 m² | 57 |
| **Children's rooms combined** | **281 m² / about 3,025 sq ft** | **111** |

281 m² is the sum of children's rooms, not the whole nursery building. Individually labelled nursery support areas include reception 9 m², prep kitchen 19 m², headteacher's office 8 m² and nursery staff/utility room 21 m². Together with the children's rooms, these identified areas total 338 m² (about 3,638 sq ft), before other toilets, circulation, stairs and other/shared support areas. This is a partial sum, not NIA/GIA or a measured footprint.

The nursery building also contains a 52 m² club staffroom/office and a 33 m² club store: **85 m² (about 915 sq ft) explicitly labelled for club use**. A 42 m² buggy store and 13 m² plant room are also labelled. Allocation of shared support space cannot be inferred solely from which building contains it.

The drawing's 111 children is a proposed design capacity. The Ofsted report reviewed separately recorded 95 places (OF02R); these figures must remain distinct, and the reason for the difference has not been established. The drawings do not supersede regulatory evidence.

Do not add the courtyard/external-pool subtotal (419 m²) a second time. Outdoor areas are separate from internal NIA. The scheduled external-pool figure and individual pool-room/water-area labels differ; do not assume they have identical measurement boundaries or use them interchangeably for pool costing.

The first-floor kids-club combined total is 207 m² (about 2,228 sq ft). This includes a baby room labelled 43 m², kids club 104 m², studio 34 m² and reception 11 m², with associated spaces shown on the plan. That is a separate club childcare provision from the full-day nursery opposite, not part of the nursery's 281 m² children's-room total.

Other relevant labels: restaurant 190 m² with 84 covers plus 50 on the terrace (the drawing notes 'excl beds'); ground-floor living-room café 86 m²; grab-and-go/bar 54 m²; main kitchen 61 m²; first-floor finishing kitchen 32 m². These are proposed annotated areas/covers, not validated trading capacity or revenue assumptions.

## Implications for the user's project

- Nursery provision and club crèche/kids-club provision need separate accommodation, capacity and economics. A full-day nursery does not automatically serve as flexible member childcare.
- Club staff and storage within the nursery building show why a simple division by building address would misallocate business functions. Model shared occupancy costs and support costs explicitly; the drawings do not reveal the actual accounting allocations.
- Use 2,010 m² internal NIA as a documented historical drawing benchmark for the club. Do not equate it to the historical 30,000 sq ft marketing headline, current GIA, lease area or as-built delivery. Their reconciliation remains unresolved.
- Do not divide 281 by 281 + 2,010 to claim a nursery share: nursery children's-room area and whole-club NIA are different measures. Nor can a 111-place drawing be proportionately scaled to a baby-heavy 50-place nursery without an age-mix and support-space brief.
- The earlier 8,000–12,000 sq ft combined-site allowance was superseded by the user's clarification: approximately 10,000 sq ft for the club plus nursery children's rooms of 2,000–3,000 sq ft and additional nursery support space, targeting 70–90 nursery places. Jesse's is a reference for functions and room allocations, not the target footprint or facility list. These drawings do not validate the user's £1.5 million funding assumption.
- A nursery garden is labelled at the western edge of the club ground-floor drawing. Its allocation, access arrangements and relationship with the nursery opposite require confirmation.

## Remaining verification

Obtain whole-building nursery NIA/GIA, current/as-built plans, the lease's measured demise, explanations for the 111-design/95-Ofsted capacity difference, and current external-space allocations before calculating a robust campus split or cost per place. The annotations are more useful than the undimensioned member-facing floorplans, but remain historic design evidence.
