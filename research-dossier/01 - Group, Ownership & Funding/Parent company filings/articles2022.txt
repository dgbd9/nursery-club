SHARE CAPITAL - GENERAL

In these Articles, unless the context requires otherwise, references to shares of a particular class
shall include shares allotted and/or issued after the Adoption Date and ranking pari passu in all
respects (or in all respects except only as to the date from which thase shares rank for dividend)
with the shares of the relevant class then in issue.

Except as otherwise provided in these Articles, the Preferred Shares, the A Ordinary Shares and
the Ordinary Shares shall rank pari passu in all respects but shall constitute separate classes of
shares.

DIVIDENDS

Any Available Profits which the Company may determine, to distribute in respect of any financial
year, will be distributed among the holders of the Equity Shares (pari passu as if they constituted
ane class of share) pro rata to their respective holdings of Equity Shares.

Subject to the CA 2006 anc these Articles, the Board may, pay interim dividends if justified by the
Available Profits in respect of the relevant period.

Every dividend shall accrue on a daily basis assuming a 365 day year. All dividends are expressed
net and shall be paid in cash.

redemption ar purchase of Shares) (a “Liquidation”) the surplus assets of the Company remaining

distributed (to the extent that the Company is lawfully permitted to do so} in the following

there are insufficient surplus assets to pay the whole of the Preferred Amount, the
Liquidation Surplus shall be distributed to the Preferred Shareholders pro rata to the
number of Preferred Shares held;

ISSUE OF NEW SHARES: PRE-EMPTION

Subject to Article 8.2 and article 8.4 any new Shares from time to time created shall before they
are issued to any third party be offered to the holders of Shares which are the same class as the
new Shares being issued pro-rata in proportion to the number of that class of Share held.

If the provisions of Article 8.5 apply, any new Shares shall be offered pro rata in proportion to the
number of Shares held (and for the purposes of this Article 8.2 the Equity Shares shall be treated
as if they were the same class of share).

The offer shall be made by notice in writing specifying the number and class of Shares offered and
the price per Share (which shall be the same price per Share) and stating a time (not being less
than ten days or greater than tweny days) within which the offer, if not accepted, will be deemed
to be declined. After the expiration of such time, or on the receipt of an indication from the
relevant Shareholder to whom the offer is made that he declines to accept the new shares offered
or any of them, the Directors shall offer the new shares declined in like manner to the holders of
Shares which are not of the same class as the new shares being issued, in accordance with this
Article 8.3 and a second notice shall be served, containing the same details as the first notice, save
that it shall state a time (not being less than fourteen days or greater than twenty days from the
date of the second offer) within which the offer, if not accepted will deemed to be declined. If
the shares comprised in such further offers are declined or deemed to be declined the further
offers shall be withdrawn.

Instrument or the Employee Options and may in any event be disapplied in relation to any class

withheld or delayed (subject to Article 9).

LIEN

The Company shall have a first and paramount lien on every Share, which is not fully paid, for all
and any indebtedness of any holder thereof to the Company (whether a sole holder or one of two.
or more joint holders) in respect of the Shares concerned.

The Company’s lien over a Share takes priority over any third party’s interest in that Share and
extends to any dividend or other money payable by the Company in respect of that Share, and (if
the lien is enforced and the Share is sold by the Company) the proceeds of sale of that Share.

The Directors {with the consent of the Imbiba Director} may at any time decide that a Share, which
is or would otherwise be subject to the Company's lien will not be subject to it, either wholly or
in part.
