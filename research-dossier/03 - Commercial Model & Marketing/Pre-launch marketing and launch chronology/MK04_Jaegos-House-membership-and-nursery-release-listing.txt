About
Jaego’s House and Nursery is the perfect sanctuary for the whole family. Becoming a member is the best way to experience everything we have to offer. As well as getting unlimited access to the Jungle Gym and Soft Play at peak times, Jaego’s House members also have exclusive access to our members-only areas, including the Gym, Studio Classes, Crèche, Kids’ Cinema and Library – and FREE Spinning Classes for the first six months we are open. We want our members to get as much ‘kids’ free’ time as possible here so they can make the most of all the great stuff the club offers. Our onsite crèche allows them to do just that. Just for members, it is run by a dedicated, experienced team who will provide the highest standard of childcare.
