A new type of club is coming
We are so very excited about JAEGO’S HOUSE!
Someone has listened to our wildest dreams and surpassed our most ambitious wish lists by creating an innovative family club with nursery, the perfect sanctuary for the whole family to enjoy.
Overlooking the canal in Kensal Rise, Jaego’s House spans 20,000 sq ft and, from children to grown-ups, it has everyone covered.
The Jungle Gym and Soft Play make an adventure playground like no other and there’s a Kids’ Cinema for relaxation.
Parents can make the most of Gym, Library and Co-working Office while the Crèche and Childminders take care of the little ones.
There’s also Treatment rooms, Studio and Fitness Classes for kids and adults, as well as a stunning restaurant and waterside café. PLUS an Ofsted registered nursery in a beautiful Grade II listed building adjoining the club!
Now the only thing you have to ensure to not miss out on membership is that you have to REGISTER TO BE ELIGIBLE to sign up once spaces are open! REGISTER YOUR INTEREST HERE
House membership and nursery places are up for grabs from February 1!
Spaces are extremely limited and will be available on a first-come, first-served basis and released in three phases - so be quick!
