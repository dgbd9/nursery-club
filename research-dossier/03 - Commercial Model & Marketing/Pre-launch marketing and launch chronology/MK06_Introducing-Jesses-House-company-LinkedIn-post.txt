Introducing Jesse’s House Parsons Green. Following the successful launch of Jaego’s House in Kensal Rise, Jesse’s House offers the same daily escapism that parents and children have embraced at its big brother club… But like all younger siblings, it will have a personality entirely it’s own!
Be sure to register your interest in membership for Jesse’s House at www.jesseshouse.co.uk, memberships will be sold on a first come first served basis and you to be registered to be invited to buy.
We will be releasing more info on Monday 18th September, so be sure to tag your friends who might be interested in joining and stay tuned for updates on what’s happening and when!
