Family club and nursery Jesse's House to launch in Spring 2024

Date posted on DIARY directory: Monday 18th September 2023

A Mediterranean-inspired family paradise and new family club and nursery, Jesse’s House Parsons Green has announced that it will open doors in Spring 2024. Registrations for memberships are officially open at Jesseshouse.co.uk.

Following the successful launch of Jaego’s House in Kensal, Jesse’s House offers the same daily escapism that parents and children have embraced at its big brother club. With its indoor and outdoor swimming pools, it’s the perfect sanctuary for the whole family to enjoy. Prices start from £105 per adult and £35 per child. There is a reduced joining fee of £250, down from £500, that will be fully transferred into club credit, for anyone who registers their interest and buys before we open. Memberships will be open on 7 November. You must register your interest first, to then be invited to buy. People can enter Jesse’s House Parsons Green's competition and win six months of free membership to the club.
