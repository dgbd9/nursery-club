It’s fine to change your mind
New members have 14 days from their start date to try the club and make sure it feels right. If you decide it’s not for you during this time, we’ll refund your joining fee in full. See our Terms & Conditions for more info.

Why join OurHouse?
OurHouse puts everything your family needs in one place. No bouncing between soft play and playdates. No choosing between your time and their time. No taking work calls from a windy park bench.
Our family clubs bring it all together under one roof – so days flow more easily, plans feel simpler, and time feels better spent. Swim, play, work, eat, train, relax. Come with a plan, or don’t. Either way, it just works.
It’s about creating more space for the everyday moments that make family life what it is: energetic, noisy, joyful, a little messy – and genuinely fun.
We open memberships regularly on a first-come, first-served basis. Join our waitlist to be the first to hear when new spaces become available.
