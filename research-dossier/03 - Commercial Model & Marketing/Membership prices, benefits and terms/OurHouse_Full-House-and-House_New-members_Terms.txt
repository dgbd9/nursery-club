14-Day Cooling-Off Period: Your legal right to change your mind and cancel
4.1. Subject to Clause 4.4 below, the Memberships shall have a statutory 14-day cooling-off
period commencing on the day that the Membership commences (the “Statutory Cooling-
off Period”). You are entitled to cancel your Membership and receive a refund of any fees
paid by contacting us in writing during the Statutory Cooling-Off Period (the “Refund”).
Where we receive your written notice to cancel your Membership during the Statutory
Cooling-Off Period, we will endeavour to process the Refund under this Clause 4.1 as soon as
possible and within 14 days of receipt of your notice to cancel your Membership.
4.2. We reserve the right at our sole discretion to deduct from the Refund the full cost of any
promotional credits utilised by Members during the Statutory Cooling-off Period.
4.3. For the avoidance of doubt, should a Member provide written notice to us to cancel their
Membership which is received by OurHouse Group prior to end of the Statutory Cooling-Off
Period, the Contract which has been formed between us will immediately be cancelled.
4.4. If you access the club before the Statutory Cooling-Off Period has ended, you agree that if
you subsequently cancel your Membership within the Statutory Cooling-Off Period, then we
may (if we are required to do so by law) deduct from the Refund an amount to reflect your
usage of the club during the Statutory Cooling-Off Period.
Term – 6-month and 12-month Contracts
