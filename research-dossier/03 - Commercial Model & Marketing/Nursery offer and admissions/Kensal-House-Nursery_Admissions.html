Waiting List

If there are no spaces available we operate a Waiting List. It’s best to enquire as early as possible because spaces get booked up well in advance!

Offer of Admission

If a space is available, we will let you know after your tour. Offers of admission remain open for a period of 48 hours. If we haven’t heard back from you we will assume you are no longer interested in the space.

Accepting a Place

To accept a place at the nursery please read our Terms and Conditions and sign the Agreement Form. You will be invited to pay a deposit and once this is paid your child’s place at the nursery is secured. Deposits are deducted off your final invoice(s).
