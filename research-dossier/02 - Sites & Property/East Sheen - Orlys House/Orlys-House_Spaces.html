Club spaces at Orly’s

Like every House, Orly's is designed to be effortless, welcoming and fun. Our club spaces will include two swimming pools, low-tech sensory spaces and imaginative playrooms. Plus dedicated areas to seamlessly dine, work, exercise and socialise. You’ll be as keen to come as they are.

Swimming lessons with Swim School

Tick swimming lessons off your list with Swim School. Our classes are for babies and children up to 10, and run in our heated indoor pool. We follow the Swim England framework and keep class sizes small for maximum progress. You can also splash around together during our dedicated family swim sessions.
