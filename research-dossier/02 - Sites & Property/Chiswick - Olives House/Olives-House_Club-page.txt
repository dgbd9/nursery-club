Olive’s House – coming to Chiswick in Summer 2027
With the river Thames threading past leafy parks at every turn, West London’s newest family sanctuary is coming to Chiswick in Summer 2027. A family club where parents can drop in for workouts, wellness and workspaces, and children from babies to pre-teens can lean into crèches, classes and kids club. Somewhere to retreat, refuel and ramp up family time.
Registrations for Founding Family membership are officially open. Sign up to hear more and be invited to buy on Tuesday, 19 January 2027.

One monthly membership. Everything under one roof
Play spaces for them. Co-working and fitness for you. As an Olive's member, there's no schlepping between kids' activities and no paying for multiple subscriptions. Just one House, one membership and a lot less to organise.
Olive's membership starts from £150 per adult and £75 per child each month, and gives you and your family full access to everything OurHouse has to offer.
To secure membership our Founding Family release, you'll simply need to pay £100. This forms part of your fully refundable £350 joining fee - you'll pay the remaining £250 in the weeks before we open. No strings attached – if you change your mind and we’ll refund the full amount.
