Make Leo's your home
Covering over 48,000 sq ft of space, these former artist studios has been re-imagined for the whole family. While they race from the Jungle Gym to the Den & Hangout, you can swim lengths in the Lap Pool or reset in the Sauna and Salt Room.

Dive into Swim School
Swimming lessons, without the hassle. The pool is warm and the classes small, which means maximum progress – whether they’re ditching armbands for the first time or perfecting front crawl. For babies and children up to 10 years, following the official Swim England framework.
