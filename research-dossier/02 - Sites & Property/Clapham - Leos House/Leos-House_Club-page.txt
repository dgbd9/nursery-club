Leo’s House – coming to Clapham in Summer 2027
Alongside the railway archways in Clapham North, a sprawling converted warehouse, once home to creative artist studios, is starting its next chapter. Leo's is a family club built around an open courtyard, with space for parents to work, train and unwind, and crèches, classes and a kids club for children to be entertained and inspired.
Registrations for Founding Family membership are officially open. Sign up to hear more and be invited to buy on Tuesday, 26 January 2027.

One membership for the whole family
Play spaces and classes for them. Co-working, fitness and a sauna for you. As a Leo's member, there's no rushing between kids' activities and no juggling separate subscriptions. Just one House, one membership and a much shorter to-do list.
Leo's membership starts from £160 per adult and £80 per child each month, and gives you and your family full access to everything OurHouse has to offer.
To join our Founding Family release, you'll pay £100 now. This goes towards your £350 joining fee, which is fully refundable. The remaining £250 is due in the weeks before we open, and if you change your mind, we'll refund the full amount.
