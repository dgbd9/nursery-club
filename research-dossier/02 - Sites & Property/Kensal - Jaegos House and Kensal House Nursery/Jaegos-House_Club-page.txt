Jaego's House
Jaego’s is our original House, an oasis for children and parents amongst the Victorian terraces of Kensal Rise. Let your kids be kids in our Soft Play, Jungle Gym and Outdoor Playground. Play games and wind down together in the Family Living Room. Or make a beeline for the Crèche and Kids Club to reclaim some personal time.
One membership. Many perks.
Members get the run of Jaego’s. Membership unlocks kids classes, soft play, crèche, outdoor play areas, lounges, gym, fitness classes, co-working and family dining. All under one roof, and one monthly fee.
Simply choose your membership package based on the number of classes and childcare hours you’d like each month (they start at £105 per adult and £55 per child). Then, leave your details with us and our Membership team will be in touch soon after about joining the Club.
