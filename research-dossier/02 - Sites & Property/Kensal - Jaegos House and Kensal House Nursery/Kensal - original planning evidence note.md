# Jaego's House and Kensal House Nursery — original planning evidence

Researched 24 November 2026. This note concerns the **original Kensal scheme**, separately from Jesse's/Parsons. It identifies functional building and room allocations; it does not prescribe a footprint for the user's project.

## Official records recovered

Westminster application **21/06887/FULL**, linked **21/06888/LBC**, received October 2021 and granted 24 February 2022. The application names Kensal House and RB Building at 557 Harrow Road. Ofsted separately identifies the nursery as White Building, 555 Harrow Road (existing dossier source OF01).

[Official documents tab](https://idoxpa.westminster.gov.uk/online-applications/applicationDetails.do?activeTab=documents&keyVal=R0LY4ORPGXL00). The portal must be opened normally before its document links will download; direct requests initially returned 404. Seven original records have now been saved. Metadata and canonical document URLs are integrated into the main source register as KP01–KP07; the subagent manifest is also retained.

## Proposed nursery accommodation

The revised design and access statement, uploaded 15 February 2022 (KP02), gives the following annotations, visually checked against the drawings:

| Floor / label | Annotated area |
| --- | ---: |
| Ground: Nursery 1, babies room 1 | 40.0 m² |
| Ground: Nursery 1, babies room 2 | 39.0 m² |
| Ground: Nursery 2, toddlers room | 75.0 m² |
| First: Nursery 3, pre-school room 1 | 65.0 m² |
| First: Nursery 3, pre-school common room | 65.0 m² |
| First: Nursery 3, pre-school room 2 | 54.7 m² |
| **Sum of these six room annotations** | **338.7 m² / approximately 3,646 sq ft** |

Source pages: **23 (ground floor)** and **25 (first floor)**, matching the printed page numbers. These are labelled room areas in historic proposal drawings. They are **not a certified total of EYFS-qualifying activity space**, not nursery GIA, and not evidence of the final as-built arrangement or registered capacity. The common room is explicitly included in the sum. Its annotation and the adjacent stairs/circulation need checking before treating the annotations as a non-overlapping usable-area schedule. Nursery reception, toilets, changing/pantry areas and circulation also appear.

## Separate club provision

On the same original plans, the RB Building includes:

- First-floor **crèche, 136.8 m²** (approximately 1,473 sq ft), separate from the full-day nursery rooms.
- First-floor studios of **48.0 m²** and **47.6 m²**, with a **58.0 m² outdoor terrace**.
- Ground-floor **café/restaurant, 185.1 m²**, and **kitchen, 38.0 m²**.
- Further play, coworking and family space on upper floors (pp27 and 29).

The executive summary on p2 expressly assigns the nursery **and coworking** to Kensal House, with the wider club amenities in RB Building. This is useful evidence of the business functions; it does not justify allocating every cost of one building exclusively to childcare. It is a 2022 proposal, not a description of all current operations.

## Whole-site form: useful but qualified

The original application form **KP06 p5** states:

- **Site area: 2,950 m²** (land/site measure, not internal floor space).
- **Existing GIA: 2,265 m²**, approximately 24,380 sq ft, for the application naming Kensal House and RB Building.

However, its table also shows **22,650 m²** as floor area lost—an apparent entry error, visibly present in the original, not an extraction error. Do not treat the form as a reconciled floor-area schedule or use it to divide the nursery and club. It does not establish whether the 20,000 sq ft marketing headline includes any nursery, shared or external areas.

## Licensing lead and remaining gaps

Westminster's **8 May 2025** decision on **25/00949/LIPV** approves adding the Lodge to the licensed area. Indexed official minutes describe its studio, study/coworking and events uses; they record an operator statement of around 1,400 registered members, with no definition of whether that means individuals or households. This differs from the historical 1,200 marketing capacity. The direct minutes download returned 403 and the supporting public report has not been recovered, so this remains a flagged retrieval lead, not an archived complete source.

[Official licensing item](https://westminster.moderngov.co.uk/mgAi.aspx?ID=21955).

Still needed: current/as-built area schedules by building/use; the exact extent of the 20,000 sq ft headline; the club/nursery/shared-space split; any capacity definition; and the 2025 licensing premises plan. Do not infer measurements by scaling the drawings. Avoid confusing this nursery with the older similarly named nursery at 379 Ladbroke Grove in RBKC; the subject setting is in Westminster.
