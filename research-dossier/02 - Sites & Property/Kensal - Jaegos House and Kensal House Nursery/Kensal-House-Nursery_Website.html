Kensal House Nursery

At Kensal House Nursery we are dedicated to creating the best possible environment for your child. This means children receive the best care and education possible and are given the space and support to develop their unique personalities, interests and social skills.

Location

Tucked away in a peaceful enclave just off Harrow Road, our nursery offers the best of both worlds: excellent connections and a wonderfully calm setting. Overlooking the canal on one side and our neighbours at Jaego’s House on the other, it is a truly unique place. Bright, airy and beautifully renovated, it is the kind of nursery that makes you smile the moment you walk in and think, “I wish nurseries had been like this when I was little!”

Our Ethos

With the help of our incredible team we create an environment in which children are able to flourish. We have developed a bespoke educational programme to guide our children and families, based around our four core values: Education, Outdoor Learning, Nutrition and Community.

Education

We don’t just look after your children and keep them safe, we inspire and celebrate every step of their early learning journey. 

Outdoor Learning

We understand the importance of children exploring the world and believe strongly in the power of nature to ignite a child’s imagination.

Nutrition

We believe that good nutrition is the cornerstone of a child’s healthy development. We take great care to provide well-balanced and nutritious meals.

Community

We’re proud to be a cornerstone of our local community – a place where children, families, and educators come together.

Opening Hours

We are proud to be a full time nursery open from 7:30am – 6:00pm.

We only close on public bank holidays, for three Inset days per year, and for one week between Christmas and New Year.

Future Schools

What happens after Kensal House Nursery?

Navigating the school selection process, especially in London, can be complex. But there’s no need to worry. Our senior staff members are ready and well-equipped to guide you through every step. Whether it’s advising you on the best schools in the area or figuring out the best fit for your child, our team is dedicated to supporting you wholeheartedly.

We establish strong connections with the top schools and work tirelessly to prepare your children with the necessary skills for a successful transition into their next educational chapter.
