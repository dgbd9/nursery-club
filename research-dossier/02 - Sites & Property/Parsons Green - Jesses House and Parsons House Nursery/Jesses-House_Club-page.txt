Jesse’s House
With two floors of curated parent and child-friendly spaces, Jesse’s is a family sanctuary in leafy Parsons Green. Take your kids for a swim in our heated pools. Let them get loose and loud in our Soft Play and Jungle gym. Or let us entertain them in our Crèche and Kids Club while you eat a leisurely lunch.
One membership. Zero juggling.
Jesse’s members get access to our pools, kids classes, soft play, outdoor play spaces, crèche, gym, fitness classes, co-working and family dining. All under one subscription – and one roof.
Simply choose your membership package based on the number of classes and childcare hours you’d like each month (they start at £145 per adult and £70 per child). Then, leave your details with us and our Membership team will be in touch soon after about joining the Club.
