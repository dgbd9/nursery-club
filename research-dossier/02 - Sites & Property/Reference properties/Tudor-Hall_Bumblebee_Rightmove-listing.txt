Description
A rare opportunity to acquire Tudor Hall, one of High Barnets most historically significant landmarks. Dating back to approximately 1577, this Grade II listed building was originally established as the schoolhouse for the free grammar school founded under a charter granted by Queen Elizabeth I in 1573. Situated in the heart of High Barnet, the property combines exceptional heritage with impressive character and community appeal.
3583 sq ft, of commercial space, with high ceilings, parquet flooring, original beams, 2 large halls, 5 rooms, including a kitchen, 2 bathrooms / WCs, and storage. Class F1.
The building features a striking period façade and a magnificent principal hall with an abundance of original architectural detailing, reflecting its rich Elizabethan history. Offering flexible accommodation suitable for educational, cultural, community, office, exhibition, event, or heritage-related uses (subject to the necessary consents), Tudor Hall presents a unique opportunity to steward a landmark property with enduring local significance.
Located on Wood Street, the property enjoys a prominent position within High Barnet, close to local amenities, transport links, and the town centre. The hall has long been recognised as an important community asset and remains a well-known feature of Barnets historic streetscape.
