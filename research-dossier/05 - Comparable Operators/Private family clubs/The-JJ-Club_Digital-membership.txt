A members’ app for modern family life. Discover the best experiences, trusted experts, perks and a like-minded community - all in one place.
“Modern parenting is joyful, exciting and deeply rewarding — but it can also feel overwhelming. We have more choices than ever, yet many of us feel more isolated, more time-poor, and constantly questioning what’s truly worth our energy.
I created The JJ Club, inspired by my two sons Jake & Joshua, to be the platform I wish I’d had when I became a parent: trusted recommendations, meaningful connections, curated experiences and genuine community — all in one place.
More than a members’ club. A village in your pocket.”
MOLLY ALDRIDGE, THE JJ CLUB FOUNDER
