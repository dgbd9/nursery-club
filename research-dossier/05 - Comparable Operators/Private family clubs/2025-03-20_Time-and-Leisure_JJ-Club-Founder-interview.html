Discover a new family members club in SW London

Molly Aldridge on the launch of The JJ Club – a village on your doorstep.

What is the inspiration behind your business?

My two energetic boys Jake & Joshua. As a busy working mum all their lives, I longed for a space that catered for the whole family to work, rest and play – starting right here on my doorstep in SW London and rolling out.  A space where families can connect, create and calm – have time together and time independently.  A place that makes a meaningful difference to busy family lives, and the local community as a whole through our collaboration with Little Village.  A Club that connects families together and offers activities and spaces for tots through to late teens.  And when I realised it didn’t exist – I set about building it myself!

When does it open and what sets The JJ Club apart from other family members clubs?

We will be opening our first SW London Club in Spring 2026 – watch this space!

I think what sets The JJ Club Group apart is that it will be a community which makes family life that much easier and caters for babies right up to the end of adolescence – with carefully curated designated areas for all ages to enjoy. 

A luxurious, holistic space and a home away from home – with five offerings all under one roof – adults only co-working, broad health & wellness (including pool, gym, fitness studio, golf simulator & spa), stand out play kids’ areas (creche & tween lounge), fantastic restaurants (adults only and family style) plus a cinema and events space too!

It will be the place where local families can come together, build strong connections across the community, work out, relax, play and have a bit of peace & quiet too for working. Everything a busy family could need, for one monthly subscription.

What’s the digital membership all about?

As a fantastic precursor to our first flagship physical club we decided to launch thejjclub.co.uk – a digital membership club benefitting the whole family at the heart of their community. With over 125+ like-minded business partners, members enjoy exclusive perks & benefits from brands they know and love, in addition to a little black book of trusted experts and 20+ curated events per month for those important in person connections too.

Our brand partners (we call these our Friends with Benefits) include Roarsome, Chez Bruce, Reformer Collective, Millie & Maisie, Philippa Herbert, Amanda’s Action Club, Hartbeeps, TARKA, RENUE, Mindful Chef, POM London, SportsFusion, Koru Kids, Oliver Black Coaching, Dreem Distillery, Prep Professor, Haymans Gin and Turquoise Holidays to name but a few.

Starting life as a digital club & community whilst building out our first flagship physical space is an innovative way of giving back to both families and local businesses via a digital membership as we develop out our first cornerstone SW London site.  It means more families can benefit from our partners and overall offering – delivering all important connections and saving families both time and money.

Is there a special offer available for the Time and Leisure Community?

Yes of course! For a limited time only, we would love to offer your readers two months free to enjoy and explore The JJ Club and our digital membership offering first-hand (usually £15 per month). Simply register here at thejjclub.co.uk and use the code JJXTL
