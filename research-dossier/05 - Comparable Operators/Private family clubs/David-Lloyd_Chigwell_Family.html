Family activities in Chigwell

At David Lloyd Chigwell, spending quality time together is easy.

Make the most of our dedicated family changing areas before heading to the pool or enjoying a game of tennis. Fancy a family workout? Try a REBELS BATTLEBOX session or get moving with REBELS BLAZE, both designed to bring fitness and fun together. When it’s time to relax, settle down for a family lunch in our welcoming Clubroom complete with soft play area.

While you focus on your wellbeing in the gym or an exercise class, the kids can enjoy their own adventures. From the crèche and DL Holiday Club to exciting DL Kids classes, sports coaching and creative sessions like Skate Squad and Lego™ Squad there’s something for every age and interest.

Keep them active and entertained

Families have always been a central part of our Clubs and we’re proud to provide a space for all the family to enjoy here at David Lloyd Chigwell.

Spend time together or apart, bond over a shared love of tennis or catch up over dinner. We've got everything you need to stay active and entertained, all under one roof.

Fun, fitness and relaxation for the whole family

Whether it’s me-time or we-time, you’ll find it all right here at David Lloyd Chigwell.

Classes for the whole family

If you’re looking to enjoy meaningful time as a family, our Signature Classes offer the perfect way to stay active together while having fun. Parents and children can take part in the classes together, from calming SPIRIT sessions to more energetic REBELS BLAZE and BATTLEBOX classes.

Re-energise your SPIRIT in this fun filled journey for parents and children. Fusing yoga, Pilates and meditation to reset you for the day ahead.

A unique HIIT class for families with 8-13 year olds. Set to an awesome mix of music, including feature weeks with bespoke playlists, it's the ultimate immersive workout.

The ultimate outdoor workout for families. Have fun and get fitter together. Work as a team using speed, agility and balance in an action-packed workout.

Classes for kids

At David Lloyd Chigwell, our exciting range of kids’ classes, lessons and activities are designed to inspire every age and interest. Whether they’re discovering new skills or making new friends, every session combines fun with development.

From high-energy favourites like Sport Squad and Action Squad, to getting creative in Design and LEGO™ Squads, there’s something for every young explorer.

Whatever they’re into, you’ll know they’re staying active, engaged and happy.

Explore classes for kids

Clubrooms and play areas

A space that you can really use as a family. The Clubroom here at David Lloyd Chigwell has a dedicated family area and indoor soft play frame so you can keep an eye on younger kids while they play.

Make the most of our outdoor terrace for a family meal, or get them socialising with other kids at our regular games nights and Club events.

Space for teens

Older kids and teens can enjoy independence and a world of choice at David Lloyd Chigwell. With programmes like Gym Start Up and Fitness Into Teens, they’ll build confidence, stay active and develop lifelong healthy habits.

They can join in on energising classes like REBELS BATTLEBOX, improve their game with tennis lessons or go for a swim. And if they need a quiet place then the Clubroom is perfect for catching up on homework - or with friends.

Fitness, fun and freedom, all in one place.

Crèche

Time for yourself can be hard to come by, especially with little ones in tow. That’s where we come in. Our safe, welcoming crèche cares for children aged 12 weeks to 5 years, giving you the freedom to enjoy some well-deserved me-time.

Each session includes age-appropriate toys and engaging activities, all set in a secure and nurturing environment. Every member of our crèche team holds a current DBS certificate, and our childcare leaders are fully qualified professionals, so you can feel confident they’re in good hands.

You deserve time for yourself and they deserve the very best care.

Safe and reliable childcare

Family activities

David Lloyd Chigwell is a place to come together as a family, whether you're feeling active or just want to unwind and connect.

You can enjoy one of our group exercise classes, specially designed to be enjoyed as a family. Challenge yourselves to a family game of tennis or take dip in the heated pools together.

Our spacious Clubroom is the perfect place to socialise and feel at home. Share a delicious family meal, refuel after getting active or help the kids out with their homework in a comfortable space.
