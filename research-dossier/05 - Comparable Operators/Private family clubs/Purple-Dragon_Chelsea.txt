THE FLAGSHIP
Chelsea, London
A Club Designed Around Family Life
Opened in October 2011, our 15,000-square-foot Chelsea club at Grosvenor Waterside is the original Purple Dragon - it's where the magic began.
Created for modern family life, it’s a place where children and grown-ups genuinely love spending time together. For children, it’s the world’s best playroom. For adults, it’s a welcoming retreat to relax, catch up with friends, enjoy great food, and make memories with their children.
From the moment you arrive, everything is thoughtfully considered. No rushing across the city. No endless planning. No compromise between what works for children and what works for adults. Just beautifully spent time together, with everything families need under one roof.
Much of the time parents spend with their children happens in the earliest years. We believe those moments matter enormously. But we also know that play and connection don’t always come easily in modern life, especially in a city.
That’s why we design experiences that make being together feel effortless. From collaborative art projects and karaoke nights to family golf days, quiz nights and shared pool parties, our adventures are created to bring generations together through play, laughter, and discovery, helping families connect, unwind, and make lifelong memories.
Spaces to explore
Imaginarium
A magical early-years space where storytelling, sensory play and imagination bring little worlds to life.
Masterchef Kitchen
A mini cooking lab packed full of child friendly kit; from safety knives to blenders, so our little chefs can concoct like the big guys.
Music Studio
A rhythm factory packed with classical and world instruments for all ages to explore the magic of music.
Art & STEM Lab
Bring your imagination to life - craft, build and experiment, from art and design to STEM and hands-on discovery
Swimming
Make a splash in our 15m pool - always warmed to 32 degrees and the perfect place to learn to swim or improve your skills.
High Energy Zone
A space to climb, jump, roll, slide, balance, bounce, and run - burning off steam and building strength and confidence.
Simulators
Play a round of golf, challenge pro goal keepers in football or become an F1 driver and race on 120 unique tracks, all in ultra-HD.
Library
A story-filled oasis with books for all ages, from nursery classics to adventure tales young readers can't put down.
Restaurant
Eat well, together, with seasonal all-day dining options from fresh juices and breakfast to supper time treats.

Gathered Together
Our restaurant is at the heart of the club, serving simple, fresh and nutritious food alongside much-loved Purple Dragon classics and a monthly changing menu of seasonal specials designed to encourage children to explore new flavours and textures.
From relaxed brunches and leisurely lunches to family suppers and much-loved Sunday roasts, it’s a space where families come together to eat well, share stories and refuel for the next adventure.
For grown-ups, our library offers a calm corner to catch up over coffee, enjoy a meal, work remotely or simply pause for a moment, with fast Wi-Fi and a concierge team on hand to support your day. And sometimes, the greatest luxury is simply knowing your children are happy, engaged and thriving on an adventure under the watchful eye of our trusted play team.
