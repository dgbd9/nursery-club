# UK private family club competitor shortlist

Research date: 24 November 2026. Working analysis, separate from original sources. This is a focused, London-weighted shortlist for the user's Greater London/commuter-belt project, not a census of every UK operator. Current public marketing establishes an advertised offer, not membership volumes, profitability or credit quality.

## Important qualified comparators and entrants

- **b_together, St John's Wood:** Westminster's November 2021 licensing decision describes café, workspace, nursery and club lounge together (PC08). This is particularly close to the proposed nursery-led hybrid. However, the current official homepage presents nursery and parties/private dining, and the nursery page publishes care services and fees (PC06–07). A current family-club subscription and its terms were not verified. Do not infer closure or continued membership trading from this evidence alone.
- **Mumadoo, Chiswick:** current pages advertise soft play/classes, cinema/sensory space, restaurant, adult lounge and crèche/kids club. General Access is advertised at £150/month for up to four adults and one child; All Inclusive founding rate £205/month includes five crèche/after-school hours. Extra children and joining fees apply (PC09–10). Homepage and membership pages retain waitlist wording and a future-facing opening testimonial; opening date/current trading remains unconfirmed. Address is 1 Essex Place Square, also the historic Maggie & Rose Chiswick address. Address coincidence does not establish corporate continuity, acquisition or identical premises boundaries.
- **The JJ Club, SW London:** current official site offers a digital membership/app, curated events, community and partner perks (£14.99/month or £159.99/year), PC11. A March 2025 promotional founder interview announced a Spring 2026 physical flagship with coworking, restaurants, play, crèche, teen space and wellness (PC12). That was an intention; no physical opening was verified. Useful precedent for community-building before committing to premises, not evidence that digital membership converts to clubhouse demand.

## Adjacent substitutes in the intended geography

- **David Lloyd:** Chigwell publishes family dining/Clubroom, soft play, crèche, kids activities, pools and tennis (PC13). Sidcup publishes crèche and parent/baby activities (PC14). These are family leisure clubs rather than nursery-plus-social-club equivalents. Analytical implication: they may compete for the same family subscription budget and parent respite needs in Essex/southeast London, even if a proposed club has no gym or pool.
- **The Lensbury, Teddington:** published member benefits include leisure/sport/wellness, family activities, crèche/soft play, members' lounge/dining and coworking (PC15). Adjacent suburban family-club benchmark, with a materially different estate and facility mix.

## Historic comparator

**Maggie & Rose:** The Chiswick Calendar's 14 February 2024 report states Chiswick closed permanently and Kensington also closed (PC16). Keep this in the historical/failure-case set, not the active competitor list. Reported operational problems are not an established causal explanation of the closures. Nursery-company ownership, club-company performance and site outcomes require separate investigation.

## Project interpretation

For the user, prioritise Lighthouse Social for adult/child membership packaging, Cloud Twelve for parent respite and paid services, Purple Dragon for family experience/programming, and the historical b_together format for nursery-plus-club accommodation. Compare David Lloyd locally before treating a catchment as unserved. This priority is our judgement, not an operator claim.

No operator's existence or headline subscription is proof that the user's £1.5m financing envelope, £500k personal cash, 50–60+ nursery places or proposed 8,000–12,000 sq ft accommodation assumptions are feasible. Comparable nursery capacity, gross/net areas, property costs, utilisation, staffing, member retention and site cash flow remain unverified here.
