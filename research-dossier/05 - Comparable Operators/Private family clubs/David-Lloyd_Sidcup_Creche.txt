Time for you, care for them
Take a little time for yourself — we’ve got the kids covered. Our crèche welcomes children from 3 months to 5 years, with plenty of play, learning and care packed into every session. While they explore, discover and have fun, you’re free to make the most of everything the Club has to offer.
The crèche at David Lloyd Sidcup
Life with little ones can feel like a never-ending balancing act – work, family, childcare… and hopefully, a bit of time for you too. That’s where the crèche at David Lloyd Sidcup can help.
Our bright, welcoming space is packed with toys, games and guided play sessions led by our expert team. While they learn, play and make new friends, you’ve got time to enjoy the Club, whether that’s a workout, a swim or just a quiet coffee.

Our crèche
David Lloyd Sidcup's crèche offers a safe, caring space where children can play, explore and grow. We follow each child’s pace, spotting their strengths and helping them build confidence through play.
Babies can enjoy sensory toys, songs and soft play mats. For toddlers and older children, there’s plenty to get stuck into like arts and crafts, role play, themed activities and building games. And if they’re new to it all, our settling-in sessions help ease the transition, so they feel comfortable from the start.
Our qualified staff
Our crèche staff are both caring and qualified. Each member of the team holds a current DBS certificate. The crèche is headed up by at least one Level 3 Childcare-qualified member of staff. We also have a qualified first aider in the building at all times.
We make sure the whole team have the knowledge and experience required to support your child’s physical, emotional and social development.
Thoughtfully timed sessions
Our crèche is open during the day, Monday to Saturday, making it easy to enjoy some time for yourself. Sessions are thoughtfully timed to align with our adult class schedule, so you can drop off your little one and head to your favourite class with time to spare.

Activities for you and your little one
If you're looking to spend some quality time with your little one, then our classes are the a perfect way to have 'we time' while getting in a workout.
Baby Club
A relaxed session with other parents where babies can kick and giggle together in a soft play space and you can chat and socialise. Grandparents, nannies and other carers are welcome.
Parent and baby yoga
Gentle yoga moves are designed for you and your baby, so you can strengthen and tone whilst spending precious time together.
Swim Stars
Our Swim Stars baby sessions are a gentle, playful way to build water confidence. With expert instructors and plenty of splashy fun, it’s all about bonding and making the water feel like home.
Parent and child football
A great introduction to football for toddlers with their parent or carer. They will learn fundamental skills such as balancing, kicking, running and ball skills through interactive games and stories.
